Antennas

Free-space patterns of lossless 433 MHz designs, added at the node end of every link at the ray's take-off angle, and at both ends between nodes; the crew or target end stays 0 dBi. The terrain model already includes the ground. Switched and phased arrays use their best beam for each link. Isotropic is the study's assumption and the default.
